About

CookieLens

CookieLens is 100% offline: no analytics, no remote code, and your cookie data never leaves the browser.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Alt+Shift+C — open the side panel
  • Alt+Shift+E — copy this site's cookies as JSON to the clipboard
  • Alt+Shift+D — delete all cookies for this site — press twice to confirm

Shortcuts can be changed in your browser's extension shortcut settings (chrome://extensions/shortcuts).
